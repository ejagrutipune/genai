HOUSEHOLD ELECTRICITY SERVICE PLANNING — PROBLEM STATEMENT

An electricity distribution company serves 900 households. Smart-meter readings and service records provide the following information for each household:

• average daily electricity usage
• percentage of usage during peak hours
• percentage of usage at night
• weekend-to-weekday usage ratio
• maximum electrical demand
• average monthly bill
• number of power-cut complaints per year

The company wants to design a small set of suitable energy-saving programs instead of offering the same program to every household. It therefore needs to discover groups of households that have similar consumption and service patterns.

Your tasks

1. Load and inspect household_electricity_usage.csv.
2. Check for missing values, duplicate household IDs, and unsuitable modelling columns.
3. Prepare the measurements so that variables recorded on large numerical scales do not dominate the analysis.
4. Select a suitable unsupervised machine-learning method for discovering natural household groups.
5. Use visual and quantitative evidence to justify an appropriate number of groups.
6. Assign each household to a group and describe the typical characteristics of every group in the original units.
7. Visualize the groups and recommend an appropriate energy-management strategy for each pattern you discover.

Important: Household_ID is only an identifier. The file does not contain a predefined group or target variable.
