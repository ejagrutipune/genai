E-COMMERCE INVENTORY STRATEGY — PROBLEM STATEMENT

An e-commerce company manages 1,000 products. Its sales, finance, customer-service, and supplier systems provide the following annual measurements for each product:

• units sold
• average unit price
• annual revenue
• gross margin percentage
• customer return rate
• number of stock-out days
• supplier lead time
• average customer rating

Using one inventory policy for every product has caused excess stock for some items and repeated shortages for others. Management wants to discover a small number of product groups with similar commercial and supply characteristics, then design a different inventory policy for each group.

Your tasks

1. Load and examine ecommerce_product_inventory.csv.
2. Check data quality and identify columns that should not influence similarity.
3. Examine feature distributions and handle major differences in scale or skewness.
4. Select an appropriate unsupervised machine-learning technique for discovering natural product groups.
5. Justify the number of groups using quantitative metrics and visual evidence.
6. Assign every product to a group and summarize each group in the original business units.
7. Create useful visualizations and recommend an inventory strategy for each discovered pattern.

Important: Product_ID is an identifier only. No predefined product class or target variable is supplied.
